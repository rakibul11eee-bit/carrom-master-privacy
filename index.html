Privacy Policy for Carrom Master Easy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Carrom Master Easy ("the App") handles information when you use the App.

1. About the App

Carrom Master Easy is an offline carrom game. The version covered by this policy is designed to be played on the device and does not require you to create an account or connect to an online game service.

2. Information We Collect

Based on the current app bundle, the App does not request internet access and does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. The developer does not receive personal information through the App.

The App may keep game-related information or preferences locally on your device as needed for its operation. This locally stored information is not transmitted to the developer by the App.

3. Permissions

The current app bundle declares the Android VIBRATE permission, which may be used to provide vibration feedback during gameplay. The App does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or location.

4. Sharing and Sale of Information

The current version does not send personal information to the developer or share it with third parties through the App. The developer does not sell personal information.

5. Internet, Advertising and Analytics

The current version is designed for offline play. It does not contain ads or an in-app analytics service. If these features or other online services are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before or when those features are introduced, as applicable.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Game-related information, if stored, remains locally on your device. You can remove the App and its locally stored app data through your device settings. Android device backup and restore features are controlled by your device and account settings; their behavior may vary by device.

7. Children's Privacy

The current version does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. The App does not provide an account-registration or online communication feature.

8. Security

Because the current version is designed for offline play and does not transmit app data to the developer, the developer does not operate an app server for storing player information. Please keep your device protected using the security features provided by its operating system.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the App's features or data practices change. The updated policy will be published at this same URL with a revised effective date.
